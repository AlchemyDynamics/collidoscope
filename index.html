🔬 HOW TO RUN YOUR COLLIDER

  1. ⚡ Set your beam energy. E = mc² — you can only create particles you have enough energy for. Heavier particles need bigger smashes!
  2. 🎚️ Crank the intensity. Rare particles are RARE — a Higgs appears in ~1 of 10 billion collisions at CERN. More intensity = better odds.
  3. 🔥 Hit FIRE. Two proton beams race around the ring at 99.9999991% the speed of light and SMASH in the middle of your detector.
  4. 🔍 Click the ? markers in the 3D view to identify particles. First time you identify a species, it's logged in your Particle-Dex!
  5. 🧲 Read the tracks like a physicist: tight curls = low momentum · straight stiff tracks = high momentum · tracks that stop in green = electrons & photons · orange = hadrons · tracks that escape everything = muons · dashed arrow = a neutrino stole energy!
  6. 👑 The endgame: discover the W, Z, and top quark, then push past 5 TeV at high intensity to hunt the legendary Higgs boson. One candidate isn't proof — you need 5σ certainty, just like CERN in 2012.

Every particle in this game is real. Every mass, every discovery date, every decay — that's the actual Standard Model of particle physics. 🌌
